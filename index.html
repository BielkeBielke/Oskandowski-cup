How the dice work

You decide every result — by hand, or with the dice. Here's what each die does.

d4 — the goal die Sets each side's goals in a match: 0, 1, 2 or 3. Also used in extra time — a roll of 1 scores.
hidden d6 — the bonus die Roll a 3 on the d4 and a hidden d6 rolls behind the scenes. On a 6 you get a bonus goal, chaining on further 6s. You only see the final score. Hard cap: 9.
d20 — the golden chance In a knockout tie still level after extra time, each team rolls a d20. Only a 20 scores — rare. One team on 20 wins instantly; if neither hits 20 (or both do), it's penalties.
Level knockout tie? Extra time → d20 golden chance → penalty shootout, in that order, until someone wins.
